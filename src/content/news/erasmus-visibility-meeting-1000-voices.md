The Erasmus+ Visibility Meeting in Meppel focused on a question that is both practical and political: how can we make the impact of youth mobility visible while decisions are being made about the next EU long-term budget?

Programmes such as Erasmus+ and the European Solidarity Corps create life-changing opportunities, but their value is sometimes reduced to figures, policy language, or institutional reports. During the meeting, we developed a campaign that starts somewhere else—with the people who took part.

## 1000 voices: 1000 real stories

We are collecting 1,000 real stories on a shared Padlet map. Each contribution combines a name, a photograph or video, and a short testimony explaining how Erasmus+ or the European Solidarity Corps made a concrete difference.

The purpose is to show decision-makers what youth mobility looks like in real life: the confidence gained, the skills developed, the friendships built, the opportunities discovered, and the decisions people made afterwards.

## How to contribute

1. Open the Padlet map and add a new post.
2. Select the place you are **from**, rather than the destination of your mobility.
3. Title the post “Your name to destination country”.
4. Add a photograph or video of yourself during the activity. If other people appear, make sure you have their consent.
5. Tell a specific story: what changed, what you gained, and why the experience matters.

[Add your story to the 1000 Voices map](https://padlet.com/erasmusvoices/erasmus-youth-stories-4on4ez8fe4k8bcya)

## Why individual stories matter

Large budget decisions can feel distant, but they determine whether future participants and grassroots youth organisations will still be able to access these opportunities. A personal story can make that impact understandable in a way that an abstract budget line cannot.

The collected voices are intended to support conversations with politicians, decision-makers, and key stakeholders. The message is direct: protect and strengthen Erasmus+ and the European Solidarity Corps, and keep youth mobility accessible to the people and communities it is meant to serve.
