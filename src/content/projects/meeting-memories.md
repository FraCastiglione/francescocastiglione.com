## The project

*Meeting Memories: Learning from the Past to Confront Dehumanization Today* took place in Ljubljana from 7 to 10 April 2022.

## What I explored

The seminar brought together formal and non-formal approaches to Holocaust education. Participants exchanged practices, considered educational materials, and explored how international cooperation can help connect historical memory with contemporary questions of dignity and human rights.
