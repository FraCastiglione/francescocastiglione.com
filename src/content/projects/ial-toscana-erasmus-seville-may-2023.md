## The mobility

In May 2023, I worked with IAL Toscana as a school tutor during an Erasmus+ vocational-education mobility in Seville. The local hosting partner was [AMT Spain](https://www.amt-spain.com/en/), which coordinated internship placements and the programme on the ground.

## My role

I helped participants settle into the city and their workplaces, monitored the progress of their internships, and maintained contact with the local partner. The assignment also involved practical support, group logistics, documentation, and responding to welfare or organisational needs as they emerged.

This was one of several IAL Toscana assignments in Seville, each represented separately in the portfolio so that the project record and map reflect the different mobility periods rather than merging them into a single entry.
