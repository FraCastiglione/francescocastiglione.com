## The assignment

Alongside the May 2023 Erasmus+ mobility and the February 2024 *Take Off* project documented separately, I completed another school-tutor assignment with IAL Toscana in Seville. This entry keeps that period visible as a distinct experience without publishing private operational records.

## My role

I supported vocational students through the practical and educational dimensions of their time abroad: orientation, workplace integration, placement monitoring, communication with the local partner, group logistics, wellbeing, and assistance when unexpected situations arose.

The repeated work in Seville deepened my familiarity with the city and the local mobility environment, while each new group still required careful preparation, clear communication, and a support approach adapted to its needs.
