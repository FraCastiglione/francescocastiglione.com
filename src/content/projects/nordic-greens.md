## The project

The full project title was *European Messengers for Youth Empowerment and Communal Wellbeing (Nordic Greens)*. It took place in Inari from 1 to 14 August 2021.

## My contribution

My tasks combined practical outdoor work with the development of activities for children and young people, working as part of an international volunteer group in Finnish Lapland.
