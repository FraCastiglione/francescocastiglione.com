## The project

Mediathon combined online preparation with an in-person training course in Tallinn from 15 to 21 July 2024.

## What I developed

Through hackathon-style activities, roundtables, and media visits, the programme examined how organisations communicate their work, make project results visible, and plan effective dissemination.
