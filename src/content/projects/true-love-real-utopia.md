## The project

The full title of the exchange was *True Love — Real Utopia: Changing Me. Changing Us.* It took place in Weimar from 13 to 18 March 2022.

## The experience

The programme used participatory and creative learning to explore love, sexuality, relationships, and their social impact. It connected personal reflection with political visions, social movements, and collaboration in an international group.
