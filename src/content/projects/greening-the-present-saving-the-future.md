## The project

This 42-hour Erasmus+ training course took place in Filaga, Sicily, from 29 April to 7 May 2022.

## What I developed

The programme covered group management, leadership and decision-making, conflict resolution, intercultural learning, Youthpass key competences, and European opportunities. It connected environmental themes with the practical work of supporting people and projects.
