## The assignments

Since 2022, I have worked with IAL Toscana as a school tutor for international vocational-education mobilities. My assignments have included at least three periods in Seville, two in Heraklion, and one in Prague.

The mobility portfolio has combined Erasmus+ projects with *Take Off*, a transnational internship initiative financed by the Tuscany Region through the European Social Fund Plus. The local hosting partners were [AMT Spain](https://www.amt-spain.com/en/) in Seville, [MD-Hellas](https://www.md-hellas.gr/it/home-it/) in Heraklion, and [Eppas](https://www.eppas.cz/) in Prague.

## My role

I accompanied vocational students through the practical side of living and training abroad. The role combined participant support, liaison with the host organisation, placement monitoring, logistics, documentation, and assistance when unexpected situations arose.

The first days of each mobility were particularly important: helping participants understand their new environment, reach their placements, follow programme expectations, and establish a reliable channel for questions. As the mobility progressed, the work shifted toward monitoring wellbeing and learning, maintaining contact with local partners, and helping the group complete the experience safely and constructively.

The repeated assignments strengthened my ability to coordinate international groups while adapting the same core responsibilities to different cities, partners, programme rules, and participant needs.
