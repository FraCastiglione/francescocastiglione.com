## The project

The 2021 edition of Model European Union Strasbourg was delivered online across 24–26 September and 2–3 October, supported by three weeks of workshops and preparation.

## My role

I represented a Member of the European Parliament in the simulation. The debates examined proposals concerning the Just Transition Fund and terrorist content online, turning the European legislative process into a practical exercise in research, negotiation, and public speaking.
