## The project

Cross Media Europe was an Erasmus+ adult-education mobility in Strasbourg from 9 to 14 March 2025. The programme included the European Parliament, the Council of Europe, ARTE, and local media and civic-education organisations.

## What I developed

The study visit connected media literacy with democracy, human rights, and active citizenship. It strengthened my understanding of European institutions and of the ways media can support—or complicate—meaningful public participation.
