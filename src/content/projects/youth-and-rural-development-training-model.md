## The project

The activity, titled *Gençlik ve Kırsal Kalkınma Eğitim Modeli* in its original documentation, took place in Konya from 7 to 13 March 2021 within an Erasmus+ strategic partnership in the youth field.

## My participation

I took part as an active participant, contributing to the exchange of perspectives on youth work and rural development in an international learning environment.
