From 15 to 28 August 2022, the summer school brought participants to Zagreb and Rijeka for an international learning programme on sites, experiences, and narratives connected to victims and perpetrators.

Francesco participated in the programme and supported the Documenta team in organising a workshop and activities for younger participants. The experience extended the themes introduced through *Meeting Memories* into a longer, hands-on educational setting.
