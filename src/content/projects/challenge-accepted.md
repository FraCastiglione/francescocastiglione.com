## The programme

*Challenge Accepted* was a multi-stage Erasmus+ programme designed to strengthen creative problem-solving among people involved in international voluntary service. The first training course took place near Bustarviejo, Spain, from 25 February to 4 March 2023, followed by a period in which participants applied the lessons in their own organisations and communities.

## The evaluation seminar

The second in-person phase took place in Lage Vuursche from 3 to 8 July 2023 and was hosted by SIW International Volunteer Projects. It created space to evaluate the challenges addressed between March and July, compare experiences, and consolidate practical approaches to collaboration, experimentation, and problem-solving in international volunteering.
