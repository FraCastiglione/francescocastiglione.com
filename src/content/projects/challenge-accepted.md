## The project

Challenge Accepted was an Erasmus+ training course held in Lage Vuursche from 3 to 8 July 2023. It brought together people working with international voluntary-service activities.

## What I developed

The programme explored creative responses to challenges in group settings, with an emphasis on leadership, collaboration, reflection, and practical problem-solving.
