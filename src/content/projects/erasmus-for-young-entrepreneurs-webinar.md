## The session

This 90-minute information webinar took place on 25 May 2022 and was delivered by Eurodesk Italy with the Turin Chamber of Commerce.

## What I explored

The session presented Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs as a European Commission mobility and know-how exchange between new and host entrepreneurs. It covered eligibility, the role of intermediary organisations, monthly financial support, and the four main stages of the journey: application, matching, contracting, and exchange.
