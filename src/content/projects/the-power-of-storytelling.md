## The project

The Power of Storytelling took place in Roosta, Estonia, from 2 to 10 December 2021. It connected storytelling practice with youth work, community organising, and responses to online hate speech.

## What I developed

The course used simulations, role play, teamwork, and creative writing to explore transformational storytelling and its practical use in communities.
