## The project

This European Solidarity Corps placement took place in Zagreb from 1 May to 26 June 2021. It connected neighbourhood-level youth work, education, media, and intercultural exchange.

## My contribution

I created two lesson plans and delivered 45-minute workshops introducing Italy to children. I also contributed to youth-club and media activities developed with the Syncro team.
