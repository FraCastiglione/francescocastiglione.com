## The project

The second IVS4Life learning activity took place in Lage Vuursche from 19 to 24 October 2021, with 22 participants from partner organisations.

## What I developed

The course focused on cross-cultural facilitation and Forum Theatre. I worked as a storyteller in a public performance, developing confidence, public speaking, collaboration, and practical problem-solving.
