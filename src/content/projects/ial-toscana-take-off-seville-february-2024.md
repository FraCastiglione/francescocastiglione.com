## The mobility

In February 2024, I returned to Seville as a school tutor for *Take Off*, a transnational vocational-internship initiative financed by the Tuscany Region through the European Social Fund Plus. IAL Toscana organised the mobility with [AMT Spain](https://www.amt-spain.com/en/) as the local hosting partner.

## My role

I accompanied participants during the placement period, helped them understand the programme and their new environment, monitored their internships, and maintained communication with the host organisation. I also supported logistics, documentation, wellbeing, and the resolution of practical issues.

Returning to the same city through a different funding framework was a valuable reminder that successful mobility depends on both local knowledge and careful attention to the rules, objectives, and participant group of each individual project.
