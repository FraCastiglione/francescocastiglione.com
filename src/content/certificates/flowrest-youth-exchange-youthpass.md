The source Youthpass was reviewed to confirm participation in the Flowrest Youth Exchange in Poland from 29 July to 7 August 2025. It records learning connected with wellbeing, nature, sustainable lifestyles, intercultural competence, creativity, digital balance, and action for social and ecological change. The working language was English, and the participant group came from Bulgaria, Italy, Latvia, and Poland.
